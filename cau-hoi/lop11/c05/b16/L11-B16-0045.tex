\begin{ex}
$\lim\limits_{x\to5^+}\dfrac{|10-2x|}{x^2-6x+5}$ là
\choice{$+\infty$}{$0$}{$-\dfrac12$}{\True $\dfrac12$}
\loigiai{\sloppy Khi $x\to5^+$: $10-2x<0$ nên $|10-2x|=2(x-5)$; $x^2-6x+5=(x-1)(x-5)$. Biểu thức $=\dfrac2{x-1}\to\dfrac12$.}
\end{ex}
