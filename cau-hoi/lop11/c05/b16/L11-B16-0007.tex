\begin{ex}
Tính các giới hạn sau.

a) $\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\left(\sqrt{x^2+3x}-\sqrt{x^2+4x}\right)$;

b) $\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\left(\sqrt[3]{2x-1}-\sqrt[3]{2x+1}\right)$.
\loigiai{\sloppy a) \[\sqrt{x^2+3x}-\sqrt{x^2+4x}=\dfrac{-x}{\sqrt{x^2+3x}+\sqrt{x^2+4x}}=\dfrac{-1}{\sqrt{1+\frac3x}+\sqrt{1+\frac4x}}\to-\dfrac12.\]

b) Đặt $a=\sqrt[3]{2x-1}$, $b=\sqrt[3]{2x+1}$: $a-b=\dfrac{a^3-b^3}{a^2+ab+b^2}=\dfrac{-2}{a^2+ab+b^2}\to0$ vì mẫu $\to+\infty$.}
\end{ex}
