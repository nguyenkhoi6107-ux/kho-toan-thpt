\begin{ex}
Biết $\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\left(\sqrt{4x^2-3x+1}-(ax+b)\right)=0$. Tính $a-4b$.
\choice{$3$}{\True $5$}{$-1$}{$2$}
\loigiai{\sloppy $\sqrt{4x^2-3x+1}=x\sqrt{4-\frac3x+\frac1{x^2}}$ nên $a=2$. Khi đó $\sqrt{4x^2-3x+1}-2x=\dfrac{-3x+1}{\sqrt{4x^2-3x+1}+2x}\to-\dfrac34=b$. Vậy $a-4b=2+3=5$.}
\end{ex}
