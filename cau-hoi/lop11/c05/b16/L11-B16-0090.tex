\begin{ex}
Tính giới hạn $A=\lim\limits_{x\to2}\left(\dfrac1{x-2}-\dfrac{12}{x^3-8}\right)$.
\loigiai{Quy đồng: $\dfrac{x^2+2x+4-12}{x^3-8}=\dfrac{(x-2)(x+4)}{(x-2)(x^2+2x+4)}=\dfrac{x+4}{x^2+2x+4}$. Vậy $A=\dfrac6{12}=\dfrac12$.}
\end{ex}
