\begin{ex}
Tính giới hạn $L=\lim\left(\sqrt{n^4+n^2}-\sqrt[3]{n^6+1}\right)$.
\choice{$+\infty$}{$\dfrac54$}{\True $\dfrac12$}{$-\dfrac53$}
\loigiai{\sloppy $\sqrt{n^4+n^2}-n^2=\dfrac{n^2}{\sqrt{n^4+n^2}+n^2}\to\dfrac12$ và $\sqrt[3]{n^6+1}-n^2=\dfrac{1}{B^2+n^2B+n^4}\to0$ (với $B=\sqrt[3]{n^6+1}$). Vậy $L=\dfrac12-0=\dfrac12$.}
\end{ex}
