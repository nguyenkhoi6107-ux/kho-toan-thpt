\begin{ex}
Tính giới hạn $L=\lim\left(\sqrt[3]{n-n^3}+n+2\right)$.
\choice{$+\infty$}{\True $2$}{$1$}{$\dfrac12$}
\loigiai{\sloppy Đặt $A=\sqrt[3]{n-n^3}$. $A+n=\dfrac{n-n^3+n^3}{A^2-nA+n^2}=\dfrac{n}{A^2-nA+n^2}\to0$ (mẫu $\sim3n^2$). Vậy $L=0+2=2$.}
\end{ex}
