\begin{ex}
Tính giới hạn $L=\lim\left(\sqrt[3]{8n^3+3n^2-4}-2n+6\right)$.
\choice{$+\infty$}{\True $\dfrac{25}4$}{$\dfrac{53}2$}{$\dfrac12$}
\loigiai{\sloppy Đặt $A=\sqrt[3]{8n^3+3n^2-4}$. $A-2n=\dfrac{3n^2-4}{A^2+2nA+4n^2}\to\dfrac3{12}=\dfrac14$. Vậy $L=\dfrac14+6=\dfrac{25}4$.}
\end{ex}
